Nay Thit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Nay Thit processes account identifiers, profile choices, selected news categories, listening progress, device notification tokens, purchase entitlement status, and reports you submit to provide and secure the service.

Google sign-in is used for authentication. Supabase provides authentication, database, and storage. RevenueCat processes subscription status. Firebase delivers opted-in notifications. Advertising providers may process device and ad interaction data when ads are enabled. Their policies also apply.

We do not sell personal data. Service-role data is access-controlled and operational records are retained only for bounded periods. Briefing history remains until you delete your account or a stated product retention rule applies.

You can update profile choices or request deletion inside Settings. Deletion removes application data, stored audio, and the authentication account after a short recovery window. Offline copies already downloaded to a device must be removed on that device.

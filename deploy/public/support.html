Nay Thit Support

For account, billing, news-source, or audio issues, email support@morningbrief.app. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or payment-card details.

Delete your account

In the app, open Settings → Account and security → Delete account. If you cannot access the app, email support from the Google account used to sign in with the subject “Delete Nay Thit account”. We will verify ownership before initiating deletion.

Deletion covers profile data, preferences, briefing records, stored audio, notification tokens, and authentication. Fraud-prevention or billing records may be retained only when legally required.
